---
title: Could "empowerment"-seeking behavior become "dis-empowering"?
---
Empowerment (Klyubin et al., 2005) was originally formulated in ML theory as the maximum mutual information between an agent's current actions and its resulting future states.

This idea has entered the CoCoSci zeitgeist, where researchers are starting to show that people may use this measure to build causal models of the world (Yiu et al., 2026), promote exploration (Brändle et al., 2023), or even determine what is "worth representing" (Dai et al., 2026).

This sounds too good to be true.

There's no way that solely relying on this "proxy" for "preparedness" would lead to states we'd actually *want* to be in, or to storing concepts we'd want or need to know.

People may be "dis-empowered" by empowerment, in that they've taken actions to explore states that they later *realize* they may never have wanted to be in to begin with.
