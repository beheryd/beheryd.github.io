---
title: What makes a pun a pun?
---
- "Whiteboards are remarkable."
- "The shovel was a ground-breaking invention."
- "An untalented gymnast walks into a bar."

In each of these poor puns I found on Reddit, we know that there exists some ambiguity in what we'd consider the "punchline". The word "remarkable" could point both to the quality of the whiteboard and to the fact that it is physically re-markable.

The cool thing about puns compared to other ambiguous sentences is that there is no ambiguity in the ambiguity. We know that the word "remarkable" is supposed to mean two things at once in the same context! That's pretty nifty.
